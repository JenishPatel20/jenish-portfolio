Effect 05 · Parallax Images

The image inside moves slower.

image slides inside card

How it works — step by step

1
The card is a fixed container with overflow: hidden
2
The image inside is taller than the card (148% height)
3
As you scroll, the image translates at only ~60% of scroll speed
4
The card moves fully — the image lags behind, revealing different parts

Result: looks like looking through a window at a scene — the scene has depth.

Effect 06 · Magnetic Cursor

Move near these buttons.

Within ~88px of any button, your cursor gets pulled toward its center. The button also nudges slightly toward you. The pull is proportional to distance — the closer you are, the stronger the pull. It feels physical.

Magnetic Zone — move slowly near each button
🧲
Cursor is pulled 38% When your cursor enters the 88px radius around a button, it lerps toward the button center — proportional to how close you are.
↔️
Button nudges back 22% The button itself shifts slightly toward your cursor. When you leave, it springs back with a slight overshoot.
⚡
Runs in requestAnimationFrame Same physics loop as Lenis — lerp factor 0.11. Zero jank, buttery 60fps even on mid-range hardware.
🎯
Best used on CTAs only On your portfolio: "View Projects", "Case Study", "Resume" buttons. Not every element — that's overwhelming.
